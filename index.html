Hashing and integrity








A hash cannot be decrypted. SHA-256 alone is not a password-storage function — use Argon2id, scrypt or tuned PBKDF2. Browser timing is no side-channel guarantee.

Classical ciphers






Caesar shifts letters; Vigenère repeats a keyword; XOR combines bytes with a short key. All are trivially broken — never protect real data with them.



Digital signatures (ECDSA P-256 / SHA-256)






Edit the message after signing, then Verify: it fails. Signatures prove integrity and authenticity; they do not hide the message. The private key is never displayed.
